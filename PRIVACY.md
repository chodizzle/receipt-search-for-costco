# Privacy Policy — Receipt Search for Costco

_Effective October 18, 2026_

Receipt Search for Costco ("the extension") is an independent browser extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Costco Wholesale Corporation.

## What the extension accesses

When you click **Sync**, or when auto-sync runs on Costco's Orders & Purchases page, the extension reads your purchase history from Costco using the session you are already signed in with on costco.com or costco.ca:

- Warehouse and gas station receipts: date, warehouse, items, item numbers, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, totals, receipt barcode.
- Online orders: order number, date, status, items, item numbers, quantities, prices, totals.

To make these requests, it reads Costco's existing login token from the Costco page. It does not see, collect, or store your Costco password.

## Where your data goes

- **It stays in your browser.** Purchase history is saved in the extension's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device.
- **It is only sent to Costco.** The only network requests the extension makes go to Costco's own servers, to fetch your history.
- **No one else gets it.** The extension has no servers of its own and uses no analytics, tracking, or ads. It does not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone.
- The extension does not use your data for anything except showing and searching your purchase history, and exporting it to a CSV file when you ask.

## Your control

- **CSV export** saves a file to your computer only when you click Export CSV.
- **Deleting your data:** removing the extension deletes all of its stored data.
- **Auto-sync** can be turned off in the extension's Settings.
